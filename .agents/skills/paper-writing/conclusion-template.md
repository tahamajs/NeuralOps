# Conclusion Template

## Structure

1. **Summary** (2 sentences)
2. **Results recap table**
3. **3-5 key takeaways** (bullets)
4. **Future work** — short-term (3 items) and long-term (3 items)
5. **Closing remarks**
6. **Acknowledgments**
7. **Author contributions**
8. **Conflict of interest**

## Rules

- No new results in conclusion
- No new citations in conclusion
- Take-aways must echo specific findings, not generalities
