### Section 2.1 — Attention Mechanism Taxonomy (131 Modules)
Source: `AUDIT_03_MACRO_TELEMETRY_AND_HARDWARE_BENCHMARKS.md` (Table 4 and Section 2).
- Classify all 131 registered attention architectures into the 7 canonical mathematical families:
  1. **Exact Softmax & Flash Tiling**: Standard scaled dot-product, FlashAttention-1/2, FlashDecoding. Mathematical formulation:
     $$\mathrm{Attn}(Q, K, V) = \mathrm{softmax}\left(\frac{Q K^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right) V$$
  2. **Recurrent & State-Space Models (SSM)**: Mamba, RWKV, RetNet, S4, Linear RNNs. Recurrence formulation:
     $$h_t = A_t h_{t-1} + B_t x_t, \quad y_t = C_t h_t + D_t x_t$$
  3. **Kernelized & Random Feature Maps**: Performer, Cosformer, Random Subspace Attention. Linearization:
     $$\mathrm{Attn}(Q, K, V) \approx (\phi(Q) \phi(K)^\top) V = \phi(Q) (\phi(K)^\top V)$$
  4. **Low-Rank & Latent KV Projections**: Linformer, Multi-Head Latent Attention (MLA), LoRA Attention.
  5. **Sparse & Adaptive Block Patterns**: BigBird, Longformer, Sparse Sinkhorn, Splash Attention.
  6. **Differential & Multi-Scale Attention**: Differential Transformer, Multi-Scale Retention.
  7. **Hybrid & Segmented Schemes**: Griffin SSD, Hymba TransX, Mamba-Attention Hybrids.

### Section 2.3 — Mathematical Theory of SVD and Low-Rank Factorization
Source: `AUDIT_02_SVD_LOWRANK_AND_RANK_SWEEPS.md` (Section 2 and Equation 3).
- State the classic Eckart-Young-Mirsky matrix approximation theorem:
  $$\min_{\mathrm{rank}(A_k) \le k} \|A - A_k\|_F = \sqrt{\sum_{i=k+1}^d \sigma_i^2}$$
- Formulate the activation covariance whitening problem: given input token representations $X \in \mathbb{R}^{T \times d}$, compute the empirical covariance $\Sigma_{XX} = \frac{1}{T} X^\top X$.
- Detail the Cholesky factor whitening transformation:
  $$\Sigma_{XX} = L L^\top \implies \widetilde{W} = W L$$

### Section 2.4 — The Hardware Roofline Operational Model
Source: `AUDIT_03_MACRO_TELEMETRY_AND_HARDWARE_BENCHMARKS.md` (Section "Hardware Roofline").
- Detail the Williams Roofline formulation:
  $$\text{Attainable Performance (TFLOP/s)} = \min\left(P_{\text{peak}}, I \times B_{\text{peak}}\right)$$
  where $I$ is arithmetic intensity (FLOP/Byte), $P_{\text{peak}} = 35.3\text{ TFLOP/s}$ (FP32) / $70.6\text{ TFLOP/s}$ (FP16), and $B_{\text{peak}} = 255.8\text{ GB/s}$ on RTX 4070 (SM 8.9).
- Prove that standard attention operates at $I \le 183.1\text{ FLOP/Byte}$, placing it squarely below the hardware balance knee point ($I_{\text{knee}} = 276.0\text{ FLOP/Byte}$).
