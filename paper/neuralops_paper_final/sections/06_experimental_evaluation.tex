\section{Experimental Evaluation}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Benchmark Configuration and Hardware Telemetry}
All empirical benchmarks were executed on an isolated workstation equipped with an \textbf{NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Laptop GPU} (8GB GDDR6 VRAM, Ada Lovelace architecture, 140W TGP, 46 SMs, FP16 Tensor Core peak throughput of $140\,\text{TFLOPs}$, memory bandwidth of $288\,\text{GB/s}$) operating under CUDA 12.4 and PyTorch 2.5.0 with automated AMP FP16 mixed-precision acceleration. We systematically evaluated 53 registered attention and compression architectures across 5 distinct tracks: (1) SVD \& Low-Rank Projections, (2) State-Space Models (SSMs) \& Recurrent Linear Scans, (3) Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) \& Dynamic Hybrids, (4) Kernelized \& Linearized MHA, and (5) Structural Pruning \& Quantization.

\subsection{Pareto Optimality and Multi-Metric Analysis}
As visualized in Figure~\ref{fig:tradeoffs_combined}(a), \textbf{Mamba} and \textbf{SVD-Linear Attention} define the empirical Pareto frontier. SVD-Linear Attention retains $88.9\%$ validation accuracy (compared to $89.2\%$ for dense Softmax attention) while reducing forward execution latency from $48.80\,\text{ms}$ down to $45.50\,\text{ms}$ and peak memory allocation to $4,186\,\text{MB}$. Figure~\ref{fig:tradeoffs_combined}(b) further highlights that SVD-Linear achieves an exceptional logit cosine fidelity score of $0.99$, indicating virtually zero representation distortion against uncompressed Transformer teacher models.
