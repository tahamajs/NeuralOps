# Appendix E. Ethical Considerations

## E.1 Broader Impact

### Positive
- Contributes to hybrid attention research.
- Demonstrates stable linear attention.
- Multi-architecture support.

### Negative
- Cumsum memory footprint limits deployment.
- Slower at short L.
- Modest accuracy gain.
