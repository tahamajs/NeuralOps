\section{Appendix A. Mathematical Derivations}
\subsection{A.1 Multi-Head Latent Attention Formulation}
Multi-Head Latent Attention introduces low-rank latent compression to alleviate the memory bandwidth bottlenecks of the key-value (KV) cache in autoregressive generation.
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{c}_{KV} = X W_{DKV}
\end{equation}
Reconstruction follows via up-projection:
\begin{equation}
K = \mathbf{c}_{KV} W_{UK}, \quad V = \mathbf{c}_{KV} W_{UV}
\end{equation}
During generation decoding, matrix associative properties permit absorbing up-projection directly into query tensors:
\begin{equation}
S_{h, ij} = \frac{(Q_{h, i} W_{UK, h}^\top) \, \mathbf{c}_{KV, j}^\top}{\sqrt{d_h}}
\end{equation}
