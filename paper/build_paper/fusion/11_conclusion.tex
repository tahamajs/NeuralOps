\section{Conclusion}

\subsection{Summary}

We presented \textbf{FUSIONATTN}, a length-aware hybrid attention with 
short-sequence fast path and content-aware block sparsity for long 
sequences. The module matches pretrained accuracy at $L = 128$ but does 
not exercise its sparsity machinery at that length.

\subsection{Key Takeaways}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Length-aware dispatch} preserves short-sequence behavior.
\item \textbf{Sparsity only matters at $L \geq 256$.}
\item \textbf{Learned gate adapts to task-specific patterns.}
\item \textbf{Not a compression method.}

\end{enumerate}
