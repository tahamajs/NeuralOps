\subsection{Summary}

\begin{itemize}
\item With \texttt{max\_rank=384}, no parameter reduction (baseline-equivalent).
\item With \texttt{max\_rank=128}, ~1.5$\times$ compression.
\item Decoder fallback preserves accuracy but not complexity.
\end{itemize}
