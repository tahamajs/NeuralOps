\section{Conclusion}

\subsection{Summary}

We presented \textbf{BAM-UMoE}, a capacity expansion method for post-hoc MoE 
application to pretrained transformers. The method introduces a three-part 
step-0 preservation mechanism (zero-init experts, negative gating bias, 
frozen base) that guarantees exact forward-pass equivalence at 
initialization.

\subsection{Key Takeaways}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Step-0 preservation works} -- verified empirically.
\item \textbf{Load balancing is stable} -- auxiliary loss effective.
\item \textbf{Parameter cost is high} -- inherent to MoE.
\item \textbf{Not a compression method.}

\end{enumerate}
