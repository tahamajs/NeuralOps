\section{Conclusion}

\subsection{Summary}

We presented \textbf{Galerkin Attention}, which formulates attention as a 
Petrov-Galerkin projection onto a DCT basis, achieving $\mathcal{O}(Nrd)$ 
complexity for encoder models. Decoders fall back to standard softmax.

\subsection{Key Takeaways}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Encoder linear complexity} is achievable via basis projection.
\item \textbf{DCT is the natural basis} for attention smoothness.
\item \textbf{Decoder fallback} is necessary for causality.
\item \textbf{Not a compression method.}

\end{enumerate}
