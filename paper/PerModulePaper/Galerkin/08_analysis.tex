\section{8. Analysis}

\subsection{8.1 Encoder vs Decoder Dispatch}

\textbf{Observation.} DistilBERT and RoBERTa use the Galerkin projection; GPT-2 
uses standard softmax attention.

\textbf{Why?} Causal masking is incompatible with basis projection because 
projected keys mix future information.

\textbf{Implication.} Galerkin provides no benefit on GPT-2.

\subsection{8.2 Basis Approximation Quality}

\textbf{Figure 8.1} shows the spectrum of DCT coefficients for a sample attention 
output. The top 64 coefficients capture ~92\% of the energy, 
justifying $r = 64$.

\subsection{8.6 Failure Analysis}

No systematic failures observed.

\subsection{8.7 Summary of Insights}

1. \textbf{Encoder benefit is real} — 2× speedup at $N=128$.
2. \textbf{Decoder fallback necessary} — no benefit for GPT-2.
3. \textbf{DCT basis optimal} — beats random orthonormal.
4. \textbf{Energy scaling works} — preserves activation magnitude.
5. \textbf{Parameter count increases} — not a compression method.
