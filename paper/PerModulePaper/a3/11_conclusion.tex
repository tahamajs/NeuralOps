
\section{Conclusion}

\subsection{Summary}

We presented \textbf{A3} (Activation-Aware Adaptive factorization), a low-rank 
compression method that combines Fisher-diagonal weighting, streaming 
calibration, adaptive rank escalation, and LayerNorm recalibration. A3 
achieves \textbf{5.0$\times$--4.5$\times$} parameter reduction with 
\textbf{-2.2\%---2.9\%} accuracy drop on SST-2 across DistilBERT, 
RoBERTa, and GPT-2.

\subsection{Key Takeaways}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Real compression works:} Parameter reduction of 5.0$\times$--4.5$\times$
  is achievable with modest accuracy loss.
\item \textbf{Fisher weighting is essential:} It reduces reconstruction error by
  30\% at the same rank.
\item \textbf{LayerNorm recalibration helps:} A cheap fix that recovers 40\%
  of the accuracy drop.

\end{enumerate}
