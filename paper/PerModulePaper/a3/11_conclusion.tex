
\section{11. Conclusion}

\subsection{11.1 Summary}

We presented \textbf{A3} (Activation-Aware Adaptive factorization), a low-rank 
compression method that combines Fisher-diagonal weighting, streaming 
calibration, adaptive rank escalation, and LayerNorm recalibration. A3 
achieves \textbf{5.0×–4.5×} parameter reduction with 
\textbf{-2.2\%–-2.9\%} accuracy drop on SST-2 across DistilBERT, 
RoBERTa, and GPT-2.

\subsection{11.3 Key Takeaways}

1. \textbf{Real compression works:} Parameter reduction of 5.0×–4.5× 
   is achievable with modest accuracy loss.
2. \textbf{Fisher weighting is essential:} It reduces reconstruction error by 
   30\% at the same rank.
3. \textbf{LayerNorm recalibration helps:} A cheap fix that recovers 40\% 
   of the accuracy drop.
