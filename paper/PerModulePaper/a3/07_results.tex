\textbf{Key observations:}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Guaranteed Parameter Reduction:} Strict enforcement of $r_{\mathrm{ceiling}} \le 256$ guarantees that compressed layers never exceed original dense parameter counts, eliminating negative reduction artifacts.
\item \textbf{Accuracy Preservation:} On DistilBERT, A3 preserves 90.02\% accuracy ($\Delta -0.69\%$, F1: 0.9043). On RoBERTa, LoRA recovery reaches 83.26\% (exceeding baseline by +4.70\%, F1: 0.8460). However, on GPT-2, accuracy drops to 63.19\% ($\Delta -16.17\%$, F1: 0.7216), which fails standard quality expectations ($\Delta > 15\%$) and highlights the sensitivity of causal decoder architectures to low-rank factorization.
\item \textbf{Memory Footprint:} Peak VRAM remains strictly bounded below 575 MB across all architectures, with zero memory spikes during factorization.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Summary}

\begin{itemize}
\item A3 achieves guaranteed parameter savings (+1,245,696 parameters saved on DistilBERT, +589,824 on RoBERTa, +525,312 on GPT-2) without parameter expansion.
\item Accuracy is tightly preserved on encoder models (90.02\% on DistilBERT, $\Delta -0.69\%$; 83.26\% on RoBERTa, +4.70\%), while GPT-2 shows significant degradation (63.19\%, $\Delta -16.17\%$).
\item DistilBERT and RoBERTa pass quality gates with sub-60 second end-to-end execution, whereas GPT-2 fails ($\Delta > 15\%$) and requires further architectural investigation.
\item Fisher weighting contributes +3.6\% to +10.1\% accuracy retention compared to vanilla SVD.
\item LayerNorm recalibration prevents activation variance collapse, adding another +2.8\% to +3.1\% post-factorization gain.
\end{itemize}
