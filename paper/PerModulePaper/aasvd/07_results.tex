\textbf{Key observations:}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Parameter reduction:} \textbf{4.0$\times$} on DistilBERT.
\item \textbf{Accuracy:} drops only \textbf{-5.7\%} on DistilBERT.
\item \textbf{VRAM:} reduces by \textbf{40\%}.

\end{enumerate}
