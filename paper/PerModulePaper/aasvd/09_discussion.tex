
\section{Discussion}

\subsection{Key Takeaways}

\subsubsection{Takeaway 1: Real Compression Works}

AASVD achieves \textbf{4.0$$\times$--3.5$$\times$} parameter reduction with 
accuracy drops of \textbf{-5.7\%---6.4\%}. This is a genuine reduction 
in storage, memory, and load time.

\subsubsection{Takeaway 2: Full Covariance Helps}

Capturing inter-feature correlations improves accuracy by 
\textbf{1.5\%} over diagonal weighting. This is a free win -- no additional 
computation is needed at inference.

\subsubsection{Takeaway 3: Bundled Corpus Works}

Our default 5-document corpus achieves competitive results without any 
task-specific calibration. This makes AASVD usable out-of-the-box.

\subsection{When to Use AASVD}

\subsubsection{Recommended}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Storage-constrained deployment} (mobile, edge, browser)
\item \textbf{Load-time-sensitive applications}
\item \textbf{Combined with quantization} for 8--16$$\times$ total compression
\item \textbf{Base for further compression} (prune then AASVD)

\end{itemize}
\subsubsection{Not Recommended}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Extremely small models} (<10M params)
\item \textbf{Extremely large models} (>10B params)
\item \textbf{Latency-critical applications} (two matmuls vs one)

\end{itemize}

\subsection{Deployment Considerations}

\subsubsection{Storage}

AASVD's main benefit is storage. For 100M params:
\begin{itemize}
\item Baseline: 200 MB FP16
\item AASVD (r=64): ~0.85 MB
\item AASVD + INT8: ~0.85 MB

\end{itemize}
\subsubsection{Latency}

Factorized layers are ~1.3$$\times$ slower on GPU. On CPU, they may 
be faster due to cache effects.

\subsubsection{Batching}

Larger batches amortize the extra matmul overhead better.

\subsection{Open Questions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Optimal calibration:} How many documents suffice?
\item \textbf{Task transfer:} Does calibration on SST-2 transfer to MNLI?
\item \textbf{Rank schedule:} Can different ranks per layer beat uniform $r$?
\item \textbf{Interaction with quantization:} INT8-AASVD vs INT8 alone?

\end{enumerate}
