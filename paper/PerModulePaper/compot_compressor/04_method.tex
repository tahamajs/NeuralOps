\section{Methodology}

\subsection{Algorithmic Formulation}
The \texttt{compot_compressor} engine executes structured parameter optimization and adaptive layer replacement:
\begin{quote}\small\texttt{COMPOT: Compressed Orthogonal Dictionary Learning Compressor.
Registered in COMPRESSOR\_REGISTRY as 'compot'. Production hardened for
multi-architecture compatibility (DistilBERT, RoBERTa, GPT-2, Qwen3.5) with
reconstruction validation, Conv1D support, rank reduction bounds, and safe fallback.}\end{quote}

\subsection{Numerical Stability and Fallback Protection}
Before committing module replacements, the framework validates reconstruction error norms and parameter scaling factors. Should numerical instability arise or error ceilings be breached, safety guardrails maintain uncompressed execution or trigger localized LoRA adaptation.
