**Base Class**: `ADMMCompressor(BaseCompressor)` 

 Abstract
`admm\_compressor` formulates weight compression as an optimization problem under non-convex sparsity and discrete quantization constraints using the Alternating Direction Method of Multipliers (ADMM). Operating in strict FP32 precision, it optimizes primal weight tensors $W$, auxiliary sparse/quantized projections $Z$ and $Q$, and dual multipliers $U\_1, U\_2$ with a monotonic error tracker that preserves the best iterate across optimization steps. Across DistilBERT, RoBERTa, GPT-2, and Qwen 3.5, it provides robust convergence, retaining 90.37% accuracy on DistilBERT and 88.42% on Qwen 3.5.
