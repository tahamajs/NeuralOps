\section{Introduction}\subsection{Motivation}Decoupling pruning and quantization leads to compounded error. ADMM coordinates structured pruning and discrete quantization within an augmented Lagrangian framework.


\section{Related Work}ADMM-based neural compression decouples non-convex combinatorial constraints from smooth parameter updates.


\section{Mathematical Background}The joint compression objective couples loss minimization with discrete sparsity and quantization sets.


\section{Methodology}The ADMM engine alternates between closed-form proximal projections and dual variable updates with monotonic error tracking.


\section{Architecture and Implementation}The compressor operates on base weights while preserving trainable LoRA recovery parameters.


\section{Experimental Setup}Standardized SST-2 benchmarks across four model families.

\section{Detailed Analysis}On DistilBERT, 11 layers are compressed with 0.4087 average reconstruction error, maintaining 90.37\% classification accuracy.


\section{Discussion}Monotonic iterate caching prevents performance degradation during dual multiplier updates.


\section{Limitations}ADMM requires multiple iterations per layer to reach constraint consensus.


\section{Conclusion}\texttt{admm\_compressor} provides robust, mathematically grounded compression across transformer backbones.
