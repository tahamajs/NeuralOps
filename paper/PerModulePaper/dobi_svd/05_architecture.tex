\subsection{Target Module Selection}

Dobi-SVD with \texttt{target\_attention\_only = True} (default) targets only 
attention projections:

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
\textbf{Pattern} & \textbf{Examples} \\
\midrule
\texttt{q\_proj}, \texttt{k\_proj}, \texttt{v\_proj}, \texttt{o\_proj} & Qwen \\
\texttt{q\_lin}, \texttt{k\_lin}, \texttt{v\_lin}, \texttt{out\_lin} & DistilBERT \\
\texttt{c\_attn}, \texttt{c\_proj} & GPT-2 \\
\texttt{query}, \texttt{key}, \texttt{value} & RoBERTa \\
Anything containing \texttt{attn} or \texttt{attention} & Any \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

With \texttt{target\_attention\_only = False}, all compressible layers are targeted 
(including FFN). Non-target layers are frozen.

\subsection{DistilBERT (Flat)}

\begin{lstlisting}
distilbert.transformer.layer.0.attention
+-- q_lin  (Linear 768 $\to$ 768)
+-- k_lin  (Linear 768 $\to$ 768)
+-- v_lin  (Linear 768 $\to$ 768)
+-- out_lin (Linear 768 $\to$ 768)
\end{lstlisting}

All four are targeted. Direct SVD, no special handling.

\subsection{RoBERTa (Nested)}

\begin{lstlisting}
roberta.encoder.layer.0.attention
+-- self
|   +-- query, key, value
+-- output
    +-- dense
\end{lstlisting}

The \texttt{query, key, value} are targeted. \texttt{output.dense} may be targeted if 
\texttt{target\_attention\_only = False}.

Nested encoder detection is \textbf{not} required because Dobi-SVD operates on 
\textbf{individual linear layers}, not on the attention module as a whole. It 
simply replaces \texttt{query} $\to$ \texttt{DobiSVDLinearLayer(query)}.

\subsection{GPT-2 (Causal Conv1D)}

\begin{lstlisting}
transformer.h.0.attn
+-- c_attn  (Conv1D 768 $\to$ 2304)
+-- c_proj  (Conv1D 768 $\to$ 768)
\end{lstlisting}

\textbf{Conv1D handling:} Dobi-SVD detects Conv1D by checking:

\begin{lstlisting}
is_conv1d = "conv1d" in cls_name or hasattr(target_mod, "nf")
\end{lstlisting}

and transposes weights before SVD. The output shape is preserved 
(Conv1D stores \texttt{(in, out)}; Dobi-SVD expects \texttt{(out, in)}).

\textbf{Attention-only filtering:} \texttt{c\_attn} and \texttt{c\_proj} are in the \texttt{\_ATTN\_NAMES} 
set, so they are targeted.

\subsection{Qwen3.5 (GQA + RoPE)}

\begin{lstlisting}
model.layers.0.self_attn
+-- q_proj  (Linear 1024 $\to$ 2048)
+-- k_proj  (Linear 1024 $\to$ 512)
+-- v_proj  (Linear 1024 $\to$ 512)
+-- o_proj  (Linear 2048 $\to$ 1024)
\end{lstlisting}

Each is targeted individually. GQA is handled at the \textbf{attention level}, 
not by Dobi-SVD. The compressor just factorizes the linear layers.

\subsection{LoRALinear Wrapping}

When a target layer is inside a \texttt{LoRALinear} wrapper, Dobi-SVD preserves 
the wrapper:

\begin{lstlisting}
if hasattr(module, "base_layer"):
    module.base_layer = new_layer
elif hasattr(module, "linear") and hasattr(module, "lora_A"):
    module.linear = new_layer
else:
    replace_module(self.model, name, new_layer)
\end{lstlisting}

After replacement, LoRA parameters remain trainable:

\begin{lstlisting}
for p_name, p in module.named_parameters():
    if any(t in p_name.lower() for t in ("lora_a", "lora_b", "adapter")):
        p.requires_grad = True
\end{lstlisting}

\subsection{Injection Hook}

Dobi-SVD uses a \textbf{compressor-level API} (not an attention-level one):

\begin{lstlisting}
def dobi_factory(model, comp_config, **kwargs):
    compressor = DobiSVDCompressor(model, ...)
    compressed_model, stats = compressor.compress()
    return compressed_model

@COMPRESSOR_REGISTRY.register("inject_dobi")
def inject_dobi(model=None, layer=None, **kwargs):
    target = layer if layer is not None else model
    try:
        return dobi_factory(target, **kwargs)
    except Exception as e:
        logger.error("inject_dobi failed: %s", e)
        return None
\end{lstlisting}

\textbf{Note.} Dobi-SVD works on the \textbf{whole model} or on a \textbf{single layer}. 
For whole-model, it traverses \texttt{named\_modules()}.

\subsection{Failure Modes and Fallbacks}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
\textbf{Failure} & \textbf{Detection} & \textbf{Response} \\
\midrule
Missing bias info & Shape check & Infer from weight \\
Conv1D orientation & \texttt{hasattr(target, "nf")} & Transpose \\
Reconstruction error high & Frobenius check & Retry, then fallback \\
Near-zero Frobenius & Norm check & Skip layer \\
SVD failure & Try/except & Skip layer \\
LoRA wrapper & \texttt{hasattr(module, "base\_layer")} & Preserve wrapper \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\subsection{Interaction with LoRA Recovery Pipeline}

Dobi-SVD integrates cleanly with the framework's LoRA recovery:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Compress} target layers $\to$ \texttt{DobiSVDLinearLayer} with gates.
\item \textbf{Gates} are trainable, LoRA adapters (if wrapped) are trainable.
\item \textbf{Recovery fine-tuning} trains gates + LoRA.
\item \textbf{Post-hoc pruning} (optional) removes near-zero gates.
\item \textbf{Evaluation} uses the compressed model.
\end{enumerate}
