\subsection{Summary}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Attention parameters:} reduce by 2.4$\times$--2.4$\times$.
\item \textbf{Accuracy:} drops -2.75\%---2.98\%.
\item \textbf{Average rank:} 160 at $\tau$ = 0.92.
\item \textbf{Zero-shot} -- no calibration required.
\item \textbf{Supports T5, cross-attention, GQA, RoPE.}

\end{itemize}
