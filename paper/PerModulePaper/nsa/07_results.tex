\subsection{Summary}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Accuracy:} drops -1.2\%---1.9\%.
\item \textbf{Density:} reduced to 63\% (at $L = 128$); lower at longer $L$.
\item \textbf{No parameter reduction.}
\item \textbf{No time speedup} without sparse kernels.
\item \textbf{All models pass quality gate.}
\end{itemize}
