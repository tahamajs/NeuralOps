\section{Methodology}

\subsection{Algorithmic Formulation}
The \texttt{bonsai_pruner} engine executes structured parameter optimization and adaptive layer replacement:
\begin{quote}\small\texttt{Bonsai: Iterative Magnitude and Taylor Pruning (IMP) with Gradient Descent Recovery & [CLS] Routing Protection.
Registered in COMPRESSOR\_REGISTRY as 'bonsai' and 'bonsai\_pruner'.

Production-hardened with:
- Multi-architecture support: DistilBERT, RoBERTa, GPT-2 (Conv1D), Qwen3.5 (GQA)
- Robust activation collection & calibration forward handling (never crashes on malformed inputs)
- Safe LayerNorm recalibration with try/except guards
- GQA-aware handling (configurable skip\_attention\_qkv to preserve KV-cache alignment)
- Trainable LoRA adapter integration post-pruning (BonsaiPrunedLoRALine}\end{quote}

\subsection{Numerical Stability and Fallback Protection}
Before committing module replacements, the framework validates reconstruction error norms and parameter scaling factors. Should numerical instability arise or error ceilings be breached, safety guardrails maintain uncompressed execution or trigger localized LoRA adaptation.
