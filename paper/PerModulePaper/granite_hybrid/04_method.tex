\section{Methodology}

\subsection{Algorithmic Formulation}
The \texttt{granite_hybrid} engine executes structured parameter optimization and adaptive layer replacement:
\begin{quote}\small\texttt{Granite Hybrid Attention-SSM Layer with MoE Feed-Forward.

Combines Mamba-2 SSM recurrent layers with dense Multihead Attention (MHA) and
Top-k Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) feed-forward, with LoRA adapters and pretrained weight transfer.
Hardened across DistilBERT, RoBERTa, GPT-2, and Qwen3.5 architectures.}\end{quote}

\subsection{Numerical Stability and Fallback Protection}
Before committing module replacements, the framework validates reconstruction error norms and parameter scaling factors. Should numerical instability arise or error ceilings be breached, safety guardrails maintain uncompressed execution or trigger localized LoRA adaptation.
