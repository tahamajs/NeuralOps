\textbf{Key observations:}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Attention parameters reduce by 1.92$$\times$} (DistilBERT at $k = 128$).
\item \textbf{Accuracy drop is only -9.40\%} -- recoverable.
\item \textbf{VRAM decreases by 10\%.}

\end{enumerate}
