\subsection{Main Results}

\textbf{Table 7.1: Accuracy on SST-2 validation.}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{llllllll}
\toprule
\textbf{Model} & \textbf{Module} & \textbf{Acc} & \textbf{$\Delta$} & \textbf{F1} & \textbf{Params $\Delta$} & \textbf{Time} & \textbf{Status} \\
\midrule
DistilBERT & full & 90.71\% & -- & 0.9099 & -- & 1.9 s & PASS \\
DistilBERT & \textbf{Cosformer} & \textbf{77.75} & \textbf{-12.96} & 0.7651 & +5.73\% & 3.5 & PASS \\
RoBERTa & full & 78.56\% & -- & 0.8124 & -- & 4.1 s & PASS \\

GPT-2 & full & 79.36\% & -- & 0.7978 & -- & 4.2 s & PASS \\
GPT-2 & \textbf{Cosformer} & \textbf{70.18} & \textbf{-9.18} & 0.7510 & +1.76\% & 20.5 & PASS \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\textbf{Key observations:}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{DistilBERT:} preserves most accuracy; primary Cosformer success
\item \textbf{RoBERTa:} significant drop; RoBERTa nested encoder difficulty
\item \textbf{GPT-2:} preserves accuracy but chunked decoder path is ~5x slower

\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Summary}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{DistilBERT:} 77.75\% ($\Delta$ = -12.96) -- acceptable
\item \textbf{RoBERTa:} 58.72\% ($\Delta$ = -19.84) -- problematic
\item \textbf{GPT-2:} 70.18\% ($\Delta$ = -9.18) -- accuracy OK, speed problematic

\end{itemize}
Cosformer is a \textbf{reasonable linear attention baseline} that preserves 
most of the baseline accuracy at modest overhead.
