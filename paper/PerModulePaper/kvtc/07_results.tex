\subsection{Multi-Benchmark Task Performance}
We evaluate \texttt{kvtc} across three canonical language understanding tasks using DistilBERT-base: SST-2 (binary sentiment classification), MNLI (multi-genre natural language inference), and AG News (four-way topic categorization). All benchmarks were executed under standardized hyperparameters with rank ceiling $r_{\mathrm{ceiling}} = 256$ and quality gate $\tau = 0.40$.
