\section{Methodology}

\subsection{Algorithmic Formulation}
The \texttt{zeros_attention} engine executes structured parameter optimization and adaptive layer replacement:
\begin{quote}\small\texttt{Zero-Mean Linear Attention (ZeroS Attention).

Removes the mean from key representations and applies normalized linear attention with ReLU-based non-negative
feature maps, with multi-architecture weight transfer, trainable LoRA residual adapters, GQA, RoPE, and causal decoding.

Hardened for production across DistilBERT, RoBERTa, GPT-2, and Qwen3.5 architectures.
target\_attention\_only: Replaces self-attention modules only, preserving all FFN/MLP blocks.}\end{quote}

\subsection{Numerical Stability and Fallback Protection}
Before committing module replacements, the framework validates reconstruction error norms and parameter scaling factors. Should numerical instability arise or error ceilings be breached, safety guardrails maintain uncompressed execution or trigger localized LoRA adaptation.
