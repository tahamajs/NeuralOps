\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Motivation}
Modern large transformer architectures incur substantial compute and memory overhead during training and deployment. Monolithic projection operations in multi-head attention and feed-forward networks dominate parameter storage. The \texttt{soa_attn} module addresses these computational bottlenecks through specialized pruning, factorized projection, and quantization mechanisms.

\subsection{Architectural Positioning}
Integrated into the unified research framework, \texttt{soa_attn} encapsulates dense projection weights into high-efficiency submodules while preserving representational fidelity and numerical stability across varied backbone families.
