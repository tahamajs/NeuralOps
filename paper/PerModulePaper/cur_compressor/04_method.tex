\section{Methodology}

\subsection{Algorithmic Formulation}
The \texttt{cur_compressor} engine executes structured parameter optimization and adaptive layer replacement:
\begin{quote}\small\texttt{CUR Compressor: replaces Linear and Conv1D layers with CUR decomposition.
Uses leverage score sampling or energy sampling for column/row selection.
Registered in COMPRESSOR\_REGISTRY as 'cur' and 'cur\_compressor'.}\end{quote}

\subsection{Numerical Stability and Fallback Protection}
Before committing module replacements, the framework validates reconstruction error norms and parameter scaling factors. Should numerical instability arise or error ceilings be breached, safety guardrails maintain uncompressed execution or trigger localized LoRA adaptation.
