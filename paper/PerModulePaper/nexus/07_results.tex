\textbf{Table 7.1: Main results on SST-2.}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{lllllll}
\toprule
\textbf{Model} & \textbf{Module} & \textbf{Acc} & \textbf{$\Delta$} & \textbf{F1} & \textbf{Path} & \textbf{VRAM} \\
\midrule
DistilBERT & full & 90.71\% & -- & 0.9099 & dense & 183 MB \\
DistilBERT & \textbf{NEXUS} & \textbf{50.92} & -39.79 & 0.6748 & sparse & 346 MB MB \\
RoBERTa & full & 78.56\% & -- & 0.8124 & dense & 712 MB \\

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\textbf{Key observations:}

\begin{enumerate}
\item DistilBERT and RoBERTa use the sparse path.
\item GPT-2 falls back to dense (causal).
\item NEXUS provides \textbf{no compute savings on GPT-2}.

\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Summary}

\begin{itemize}
\item NEXUS provides sub-quadratic attention for encoders.
\item No benefit on causal decoders.
\item Several components (stripe, RFF) are inactive under our setup.
\end{itemize}
