
\section{11. Conclusion}

\subsection{11.1 Summary}

We presented \textbf{AdaSplash}, an adaptive splash attention mechanism that 
combines \textbf{$\alpha$-entmax sparse activations} with \textbf{local sliding-window masking} 
and \textbf{attention sink preservation}. AdaSplash is \textbf{not} a compression 
method -- it \textbf{increases} parameter count by ~{{PARAM\_CHANGE}}\% due to LoRA 
adapters. Its value is in producing \textbf{~40\% sparse attention} without 
accuracy loss.

\subsection{11.3 Key Takeaways}

1. \textbf{$\alpha$-entmax produces exact zeros} without accuracy loss.
2. \textbf{Attention sinks are essential} for stability.
3. \textbf{Splash masking} works for causal decoders.
4. \textbf{Not a compression method} -- parameter count increases.
5. \textbf{Slower than baseline} due to bisection.
