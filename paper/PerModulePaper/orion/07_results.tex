\textbf{Key observations:}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Parameter reduction:} 48.2\%$\times$ (DistilBERT), 45.1\%$\times$ (RoBERTa),
  46.0\%$\times$ (GPT-2).
\item \textbf{Accuracy drop:} -35.21\%, -27.64\%, -28.44\%.
\item **ORION is the first module in our framework to combine real parameter
  reduction with competitive accuracy.**

\end{enumerate}
