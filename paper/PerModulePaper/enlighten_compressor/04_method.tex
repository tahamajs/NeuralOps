\section{Methodology}

\subsection{Algorithmic Formulation}
The \texttt{enlighten_compressor} engine executes structured parameter optimization and adaptive layer replacement:
\begin{quote}\small\texttt{ENLighten: Low-Rank SVD + 2:4 Structured Sparsity (Photonic-Aligned).
Registered as 'enlighten'. Production hardened for multi-architecture compatibility
(DistilBERT, RoBERTa, GPT-2, Qwen3.5) with vectorization, error validation, and safe fallback.}\end{quote}

\subsection{Numerical Stability and Fallback Protection}
Before committing module replacements, the framework validates reconstruction error norms and parameter scaling factors. Should numerical instability arise or error ceilings be breached, safety guardrails maintain uncompressed execution or trigger localized LoRA adaptation.
