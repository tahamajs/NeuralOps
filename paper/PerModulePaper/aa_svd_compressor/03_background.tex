\section{03 Background}

 3. Mathematical Background

 3.1 Truncated SVD
Let $W \in \mathbb{R}^{d\_{\text{out}} \times d\_{\text{in}}}$. Full Singular Value Decomposition yields:
$$W = U \Sigma V^\top$$
where $U \in \mathbb{R}^{d\_{\text{out}} \times d\_{\text{out}}}$ and $V \in \mathbb{R}^{d\_{\text{in}} \times d\_{\text{in}}}$ are orthogonal matrices, and $\Sigma = \operatorname{diag}(\sigma\_1, \sigma\_2, \dots, \sigma\_k)$ contains singular values in descending order $\sigma\_1 \ge \sigma\_2 \ge \dots \ge 0$.

Truncation to rank $r < k$ selects the top $r$ singular components:
$$\hat{W} = U\_r \Sigma\_r V\_r^\top$$

 3.2 Output Error Minimization
Given input representations $X \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times d\_{\text{in}}}$, the layer output error is:
$$\mathcal{L} = \| W X^\top - \hat{W} X^\top \|\_F^2 = \operatorname{Tr}\left( (W - \hat{W}) X^\top X (W - \hat{W})^\top \right) = \| (W - \hat{W}) C\_X^{1/2} \|\_F^2$$
where $C\_X = \frac{1}{B} X^\top X \in \mathbb{R}^{d\_{\text{in}} \times d\_{\text{in}}}$ is the uncentered empirical activation covariance matrix.
