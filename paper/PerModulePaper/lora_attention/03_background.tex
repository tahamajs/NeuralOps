\section{Background}

\subsection{Attention Projections}

For input $X \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times L \times d}$:

\[
Q = X W_Q, \quad K = X W_K, \quad V = X W_V
\tag{3.1}
\]

with $W_Q \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times H d_h}$ and $W_K, W_V \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times H_{kv} d_h}$.

The output projection:

\[
\text{Out} = \text{Attn}(Q, K, V) \cdot W_O, \quad W_O \in \mathbb{R}^{H d_h \times d}
\tag{3.2}
\]

Total attention parameters: $4 d^2$ for standard MHA.

\subsection{LoRA}

LoRA (Hu et al., 2021) parameterizes weight updates as:

\[
\Delta W = \frac{\alpha}{r_L} B A
\tag{3.3}
\]

with $B \in \mathbb{R}^{d_\text{out} \times r_L}$, $A \in \mathbb{R}^{r_L \times d_\text{in}}$.

\textbf{Initialization.} $A \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2)$, $B = 0$. So 
$\Delta W = 0$ at step 0.

\textbf{Forward.} For input $x$:

\[
y = W_\text{frozen} x + \frac{\alpha}{r_L} B A x
\tag{3.4}
\]

\textbf{Mergeability.} After training, we can compute:

\[
W_\text{merged} = W_\text{frozen} + \frac{\alpha}{r_L} B A
\tag{3.5}
\]

and discard $A, B$, giving zero inference overhead.

\subsection{Trainable Parameter Count}

For each attention projection $W \in \mathbb{R}^{d_\text{out} \times d_\text{in}}$:

\[
\text{Trainable}_\text{LoRA} = r_L (d_\text{out} + d_\text{in})
\tag{3.6}
\]

For $d = 768$, $r_L = 16$:

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
\textbf{Projection} & \textbf{Frozen} & \textbf{Trainable (LoRA)} \\
\midrule
$W_Q$ & 589,824 & $16 \cdot 1536 = 24,576$ \\
$W_K$ & 589,824 & 24,576 \\
$W_V$ & 589,824 & 24,576 \\
$W_O$ & 589,824 & 24,576 \\
\textbf{Total} & 2,359,296 & \textbf{98,304} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\textbf{Reduction:} ~24$\times$ fewer trainable parameters.

\subsection{Memory Savings}

Full fine-tuning requires:
\begin{itemize}
\item Gradients: \texttt{params $\times$ 2 bytes} (FP16)
\item AdamW state: \texttt{params $\times$ 8 bytes} (two FP32 moments)
\item Total: \texttt{params $\times$ 10 bytes}

\end{itemize}
For DistilBERT attention (14.2M params):
\begin{itemize}
\item Full FT: ~142 MB of optimizer state per layer
\item LoRA FT: ~1 MB

\end{itemize}
\textbf{Total reduction: ~100$\times$}

\begin{lstlisting}

\end{lstlisting}

\begin{lstlisting}

\end{lstlisting}

\textbf{Critical.} For nested encoders, the LoRA module must \textbf{not} apply 
\texttt{out\_proj} -- the outer layer handles it.
