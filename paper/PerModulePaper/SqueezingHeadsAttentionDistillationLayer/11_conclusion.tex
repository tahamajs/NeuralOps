
\section{11. Conclusion}

\subsection{11.1 Summary}

We presented \textbf{Squeezing Heads}, a LoRA-wrapped attention module for head 
knowledge distillation. The wrapper preserves pretrained weights exactly 
and allows head-dim reduction with LoRA recovery.

\subsection{11.3 Key Takeaways}

1. \textbf{LoRA recovers most accuracy} lost by head-dim reduction.
2. \textbf{Trainable params reduced by ~100$\times$.}
3. \textbf{Drop-in wrapper} -- no architecture change.
4. \textbf{Multi-architecture support} with RoPE, GQA, causality.
5. \textbf{Beats head pruning} by not discarding information.
