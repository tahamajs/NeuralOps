
\section{Conclusion}

\subsection{Summary}

We presented \textbf{HALO-ReGLA}, a hybrid attention module that runs dense 
softmax and rectified linear streams in parallel, combined by per-head 
learnable gates. The gate is initialized to favor dense behavior and shifts 
toward linear during training.

\subsection{Key Takeaways}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Learned gates are bimodal}: dense vs linear heads.
\item \textbf{Layer depth correlates with gate.}
\item \textbf{Learned fusion beats fixed fusion.}
\item \textbf{ELU+1 is optimal kernel.}
\item \textbf{No speedup without hard routing.}

\end{enumerate}
