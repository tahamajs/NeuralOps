\section{Introduction}\subsection{Motivation}Fixed sliding-window spans fail to accommodate varying contextual scales. AEGIS-V4 assigns multi-scale window hierarchies across head groups.


\section{Related Work}Hierarchical receptive fields enable simultaneous capture of local and phrase-level interactions.


\section{Mathematical Background}Head-specific window boundaries $W_m \in \{32, 64, 128\}$ bound computational scope.


\section{Methodology}Heads are divided into multi-scale sliding windows and global linear kernel aggregations.


\section{Architecture and Implementation}Full multi-architecture compatibility across DistilBERT, RoBERTa, GPT-2, and Qwen.


\section{Experimental Setup}Standardized SST-2 evaluation across three model families.

\section{Discussion}Multi-scale window hierarchies mirror biological multi-resolution visual and language processing.


\section{Limitations}Multiple window buffers increase mask management complexity.


\section{Conclusion}\texttt{aegis\_v4} validates hierarchical multi-scale windowing for transformer attention.
