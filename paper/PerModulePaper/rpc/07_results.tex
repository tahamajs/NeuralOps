\textbf{Observation.} The theoretical speedup is not realized in practice 
because:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The projection adds overhead.
\item Kernel launch overhead dominates at $L = 128$.
\item Memory bandwidth is the bottleneck, not FLOPs.

\end{enumerate}
