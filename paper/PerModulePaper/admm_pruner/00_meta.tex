 ADMM Pruner with Distillation and Dynamic Rho (admm\_pruner)

**Registry Keys**: `admm\_pruner`, `admm\_pruner\_distill`, `admm\_pruner\_factory` 
**Source**: `Codes/framework/compression\_engine/admm\_pruner.py` (768 lines)
**Type**: Structured and Semi-Structured Pruning with Layer-Wise Distillation and Dynamic Penalty Scheduling
**Base Class**: `ADMMPruner(BaseCompressor)`

 Abstract
`admm\_pruner` optimizes structured (2:4 and block) pruning masks using an augmented Lagrangian framework coupled with layer-wise feature reconstruction distillation, Straight-Through Estimators (STE), and dynamic penalty $
ho$ scheduling based on primal and dual residual ratios. Across DistilBERT, RoBERTa, and Qwen 3.5, it prunes 36 to 72 layers with strict 50% sparsity while preserving high classification accuracy (84.29% on DistilBERT, 80.62% on RoBERTa, 78.21% on Qwen 3.5).
