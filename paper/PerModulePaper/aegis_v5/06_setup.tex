\subsection{AEGIS-V5 Hyperparameters}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
\textbf{Hyperparameter} & \textbf{Default} & \textbf{Notes} \\
\midrule
\texttt{svd\_energy} ($\eta$) & 0.95 \& SVD rank selection \\
\texttt{min\_rank} & 4 \& Floor for SVD rank \\
\texttt{max\_rank} & 384 \& Ceiling for SVD rank \\
\texttt{num\_local\_heads} & 8 \& Local attention heads \\
\texttt{num\_global\_heads} & 4 \& Global attention heads \\
\texttt{local\_window} ($W$) & 64 \& Sliding window \\
\texttt{rff\_features} ($F$) & 128 \& RFF dimension \\
\texttt{gate\_init\_bias} & 1.8 \& Prior for local (overridden to 2.0) \\
\texttt{softpick\_temperature} & 1.0 \& Attention temperature \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\textbf{Critical:} With \texttt{max\_rank = 384} and \texttt{svd\_energy = 0.95}, the effective 
SVD rank is often 384, which equals $d/2$ -- \textbf{no parameter reduction}.

For actual compression, set \texttt{max\_rank = 128} or lower.
