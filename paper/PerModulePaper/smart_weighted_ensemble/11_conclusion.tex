\section{Conclusion}
This paper formalized the architecture, mathematical formulations, and empirical evaluation of \texttt{smartweightedensemble}. By restructuring monolithic projection weights into factorized, regularized, and hardware-aligned representations, \texttt{smartweightedensemble} effectively alleviates transformer compute and memory overhead.

Empirical evaluations across encoder and decoder backbones confirm that \texttt{smartweightedensemble} achieves robust compression while maintaining high downstream accuracy. Decoupled adapter fine-tuning ensures reliable post-compression parameter recovery across distinct domain datasets and task objectives without inducing catastrophic representation degradation.

The module constitutes a verified and reproducible component in parameter-efficient deep learning. Future research will explore automated layer-wise budget allocation, non-uniform singular truncation, and cross-layer weight sharing to further diminish memory footprints in next-generation foundation models.
