 1. Executive Abstract \& Theoretical Motivation


Uniform low-rank truncation across all neural network layers causes non-uniform accuracy degradation because different layers exhibit distinct singular value decay rates and information capacities. Critical transformer projections—such as self-attention key/query matrices and MLP gate projections—suffer severe performance drops under rigid rank constraints, whereas redundant layers can withstand aggressive rank reduction.

`AdaSVDCompressor` addresses this imbalance by implementing an adaptive global rank allocation framework driven by layer-wise Nuclear Norm ($\|W\|\_*$) importance metrics, combined with alternating least-squares iterative factor refinement. By evaluating the spectral energy distribution across all linear operators in a model, `AdaSVDCompressor` dynamically assigns rank budgets proportional to each layer's information capacity. Following initial truncated SVD initialization, the engine executes alternating optimization steps to refine left- and right-singular factor matrices ($U\_r$ and $V\_r^T$), compensating for cross-layer rank truncation errors under a global parameter removal target (`target\_param\_ratio`).
