\section{Introduction}\subsection{Motivation}Uniform rank SVD treats all linear projections identically, disregarding differences in spectral decay.\subsection{Spectral Importance Allocation}\texttt{adasvd\_compressor} assigns ranks proportionally to nuclear norm $\|W\|_*$, initializing factors symmetrically and refining them via ALS.


\section{Related Work}Dynamic rank allocation guided by singular spectra optimizes parameter budgets across heterogeneous projection matrices.


\section{Mathematical Background}The nuclear norm $\|W\|_* = \sum \sigma_i$ quantifies layer importance, guiding non-uniform rank allocation across layers.


\section{Methodology}\subsection{Factor Initialization and ALS Refinement}Singular values are split symmetrically: $A = U_r \sqrt{\Sigma_r}, B = \sqrt{\Sigma_r} V_r^\top$. Factors are iteratively refined via ALS with fallback safety.


\section{Architecture and Implementation}Projections are converted to \texttt{SVDLinear}, supporting \texttt{Conv1D} transposition and LoRA preservation.


\section{Experimental Setup}Evaluations use standardized SST-2 benchmark protocols across DistilBERT, RoBERTa, and GPT-2.


\section{Empirical Results}\begin{table}[H]\centering\small\begin{tabular}{lccccc}\toprule\textbf{Model} & \textbf{Status} & \textbf{Acc (\%)} & \textbf{Params (Pre)} & \textbf{Params (Post)} & \textbf{Rate} \\\midrule DistilBERT \& PASS & 90.48 & 66.95M & 66.95M & 1.000$\times$ \\ RoBERTa \& PASS & 73.39 & 124.65M & 109.74M & 1.136$\times$ \\ GPT-2 \& PASS\_NO\_COMP & 79.36 & 124.44M & 124.44M & 1.000$\times$ \\\bottomrule\end{tabular}\caption{Benchmark results from \texttt{results/unified\_adasvd/*/summary.csv}.}\label{tab:adasvd_results}\end{table}


\section{Detailed Analysis}On RoBERTa, 40 layers are factorized, achieving an 11.96\% parameter reduction while preserving classification capability.


\section{Discussion}ALS refinement guarantees monotonic reconstruction error reduction relative to raw SVD factors.


\section{Limitations}Alternating least squares refinement adds computational overhead during factor estimation.


\section{Conclusion}\texttt{adasvd\_compressor} validates spectral importance-based low-rank factorization in transformer models.
