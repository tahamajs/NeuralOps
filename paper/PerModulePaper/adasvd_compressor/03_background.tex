 3. Mathematical Background
For weight matrix $W \in \mathbb{R}^{d\_{	ext{out}} 	imes d\_{	ext{in}}}$, the nuclear norm is $\|W\|\_* = \sum\_{i=1}^{\min(d\_{	ext{in}}, d\_{	ext{out}})} \sigma\_i$. Total model spectral capacity is distributed across layers proportional to their nuclear norms.
