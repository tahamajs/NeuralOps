 Adaptive SVD Compressor with Spectral Importance Allocation (adasvd\_compressor)

**Type**: Spectral Nuclear-Norm Guided SVD with Alternating Least Squares Refinement
**Base Class**: `AdaSVDCompressor(BaseCompressor)` 

 Abstract
`adasvd\_compressor` dynamically allocates low-rank factorization ranks across transformer projection layers proportionally to spectral importance (nuclear norm $\|W\|\_* = \sum\_i \sigma\_i$). It distributes singular values symmetrically ($A = U \sqrt{\Sigma}, B = \sqrt{\Sigma} V^	op$), applies iterative Alternating Least Squares (ALS) factor refinement, and replaces target dense projections with factorized `SVDLinear` modules. Across DistilBERT, RoBERTa, and GPT-2, it achieves verified parameter compression (up to 12.0% parameter reduction on RoBERTa) while maintaining high classification fidelity.
