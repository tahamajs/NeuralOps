\section{Methodology}

\subsection{Algorithmic Formulation}
The \texttt{kronecker_compressor} engine executes structured parameter optimization and adaptive layer replacement:
\begin{quote}\small\texttt{Kronecker Product Compressor: replaces nn.Linear and Conv1D layers with KroneckerLinear.
Registered in COMPRESSOR\_REGISTRY as 'kronecker'. Production hardened with
reconstruction validation, Conv1D support, adaptive SVD fallback, and critical layer protection.}\end{quote}

\subsection{Numerical Stability and Fallback Protection}
Before committing module replacements, the framework validates reconstruction error norms and parameter scaling factors. Should numerical instability arise or error ceilings be breached, safety guardrails maintain uncompressed execution or trigger localized LoRA adaptation.
