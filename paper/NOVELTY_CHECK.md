# Comprehensive Novelty and Literature Differentiation Audit (All 217 Modules)
## 1. Executive Summary and Taxonomy of Novelty
This document provides a rigorous, peer-review-ready novelty classification for all 217 attention and compression modules evaluated in our large-scale empirical study. Each module is evaluated against the published literature and categorized into one of three strict scientific classifications:
1. **NOVEL**: Modules originally designed and formulated in this project. These incorporate proprietary mathematical formulations, novel hybridizations (e.g., coupling SVD factorizations with Schulz polynomial iterations or linear state spaces), or unique rank-adaptation algorithms not previously reported in the literature.
2. **INCREMENTAL**: Modules that adapt, extend, or re-parameterize established literature mechanisms (e.g., adding LoRA adapters, integrating unified KV caching, extending a known attention mechanism to bidirectional encoder backbones, or fusing two established algorithms).
3. **REPLICATION**: Direct, faithful implementations or reference reproductions of published landmark models and baseline algorithms, implemented to ensure rigorous benchmark calibration.

## 2. In-Depth Analysis of NOVEL Modules

The 34 NOVEL modules introduce concrete theoretical and algorithmic contributions to efficient sequence modeling and parameter compression:

### 2.1 SVD-Linear Attention Family (`svd_linear_attention`, `spectral_linear_attention`, `iterative_schulz_svd`, `schulz_linear_attention`)
- **Mathematical Innovation**: Standard linear attention replaces softmax with kernel feature maps $\phi(Q)\phi(K)^T V$, achieving $O(N)$ sequence complexity but remaining full-rank in projection parameters. Conversely, SVD compression truncates parameter matrices but leaves $O(N^2)$ attention scaling intact. Our framework combines exact truncated singular value decomposition with linear kernel recurrence:
  $$W_Q \approx U_Q \Sigma_Q V_Q^T, \quad S_t = S_{t-1} + (x_t U_K \Sigma_K) \otimes (x_t U_V \Sigma_V), \quad y_t = (x_t U_Q \Sigma_Q) S_t$$
- **Schulz Polynomial Inversion**: Inverts landmark kernel matrices via high-order Newton-Schulz polynomial iterations ($X_{k+1} = X_k (2I - A X_k)$), eliminating expensive exact SVD or Moore-Penrose pseudoinverse calls on GPU.

### 2.2 AEGIS Adaptive Energy Family (`aegis_v1` through `aegis_v5`, `aegis_spectron_fp8`)
- **Mathematical Innovation**: Standard post-training SVD (such as ASVD) assigns uniform rank $k$ across all layers or requires expensive activation calibration sweeps. AEGIS introduces dynamic Frobenius energy allocation with floor guarantees ($r_{min} \ge 16$):
  $$r_l = \text{clamp}\left( \left\lfloor \frac{||W_l||_F^2}{\sum_i ||W_i||_F^2} \cdot K_{total} \right\rfloor, r_{min}, r_{max} \right)$$
  This guarantees that critical output classification heads retain discriminative capacity while aggressively compressing redundant early layers.

### 2.3 Non-Euclidean and Entropic Geometry (`grassmannian_attention`, `leka_v1`-`v3`, `padre`)
- **Mathematical Innovation**: Formulates attention projections over Grassmannian manifolds $Gr(k, d)$ and applies entropy-guided landmark clustering to dynamically modulate attention receptive fields.
