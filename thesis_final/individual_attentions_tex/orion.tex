\section*{۲. مبانی تئوریک و انگیزه طراحی معماری}
Standard Multi-Head Self-Attention (MHSA) in Transformer architectures requires quadratic computational time and memory scaling mathcal O (N 2) with sequence length N . While linearized kernel attention models reduce sequence complexity to mathcal O (N cdot F cdot d h) , they frequently degrade local representation quality due to over-smoothed attention distributions. Conversely, full softmax attention retains high-resolution local dependencies but suffers from memory bottlenecks on long inputs.
