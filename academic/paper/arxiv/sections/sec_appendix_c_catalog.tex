\section{Comprehensive Architectural Catalog of All 203 Unified Modules}
\label{sec:appendix_c_catalog}

This appendix provides the definitive, exhaustive technical monograph and encyclopedic architectural catalog for all 203 production modules unified within the \textsc{NeuralOps} framework. The suite spans exactly 131 attention architectures across 14 structural families (Batches 01--13) and 72 compression and surgical engines across 8 algorithmic paradigms (Batches 14--20). Every individual dossier contains full mathematical formulations, backward autograd vector-Jacobian products (VJP), exact input/output tensor contracts, operational arithmetic intensities ($I_{\text{ops}}$) grounded on the physical NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 (Ada Lovelace, AD104 silicon: 5,888 CUDA cores, 46 SMs, 184 Tensor Cores, 160.2 TFLOPS boost / 141.6 TFLOPS base, 504.0 GB/s GDDR6X, ridge point $I^* = 317.9\text{ FLOP/Byte}$), three-tier fallback dispatch specifications (Native CUDA C++ Level 1, Triton JIT Level 2, Vectorized PyTorch Level 3), and empirical execution telemetry drawn from the master benchmark ledger. The authoritative benchmark ledger and raw execution profiles are publicly accessible on Hugging Face at \url{https://huggingface.co/datasets/Hooshaai/neuralops-results}.
