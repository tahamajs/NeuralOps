    Complete Multi-Model & Cross-Dataset Benchmark Report

    This document compiles, audits, and analyzes all experimental evaluations across the full suite of attention mechanisms and compression engines.

    1. Executive Summary & Aggregate Statistics

    • Total Unique Module Configurations Evaluated: 355 (254 unique architecture bases)
    • Primary Benchmarks Included:
    • DistilBERT on SST-2 (Binary classification, 872 validation samples, baseline dense ~91.06%)
    • RoBERTa on SST-2 (12-layer bidirectional encoder, baseline dense ~94.61%)
    • DistilBERT on MNLI (3-class natural language inference, 9815 validation samples, baseline dense ~81.50%)
    • Rank-Sensitivity Ablation Sweeps (\(r \in \{16, 32, 64, 128, 256\}\))

    Aggregate Pass Rates

    • Pass all 3 datasets (DistilBERT SST-2, RoBERTa SST-2, MNLI): 110 modules (Robust generalizers)
    • Pass 2 datasets: 57 modules
    • Pass 1 dataset: 87 modules
    • Tier 1 (SOTA Tier, \(\ge 90\%\) Accuracy on SST-2): 154 configurations
    • Tier 2 (High Recovery, \(85\% - 90\%\)): 47 configurations
    • Tier 3 (Moderate Recovery / Linear Trade-off, \(70\% - 85\%\)): 27 configurations

    6. Comprehensive Findings & Research Takeaways

    1. SVD & Truncation-Aware Factorization Superiority:
    2. Methods integrating whitening and activation-weighting (soka, frandi, adasvd, svd_llm, oats) lead the benchmark, reaching 94.61% on RoBERTa and >90.5% on DistilBERT, matching uncompressed performance.
    3. Linear Attention Trade-offs:
    4. Linear attention mechanisms (galerkin, cosformer, performer_attention, hla) consistently maintain high token throughput (13k - 18k tokens/sec) and low memory (198 - 322 MB VRAM), stabilizing at 74% - 80% accuracy without full quadratic attention.
    5. Multi-Task & Multi-Domain Robustness:
    6. 110 architectures cleanly generalized across all 3 evaluation benchmarks simultaneously, showing that fine-tuned low-rank adapters effectively recover representation power across both single-sequence sentiment analysis (SST-2) and cross-sentence entailment (MNLI).
