    Academic & Empirical Bad Results Audit Report

    Date: 2026-11-01
    Auditor / Author: taha <tahamaj4@gmail.com>
    Scope: Forensic inventory and cross-referencing of negative results, failure modes, timeouts, sub-1.0x kernel latencies, unverified states, and pseudo-Triton functions across academic/, docs/, results/, README.md, and CHANGELOG.md.

    1. Executive Summary

    This report establishes a rigorous, unified audit of all known limitations, sub-optimal results, regressions, and failures within the NeuralOps platform. In strict accordance with the repository's scientific honesty guidelines, every negative result and hardware bottleneck is documented with exact provenance, correlating theoretical and textual claims in academic papers and documentation against raw empirical evidence in benchmark ledger files.

    Key Audit Findings

    1. The 203-Module Historical Ledger & Release-Gate Discrepancy:
    2. As documented in CHANGELOG.md (lines 183–186) and docs/changelog.md (lines 46–49) for release v1.5.0, the 203-module unified release gate recorded: $\(\text{PASS\_FAST}=15, \quad \text{PASS\_SLOW}=115, \quad \text{FAIL\_CORRECTNESS}=71, \quad \text{FAIL}=1, \quad \text{TIMEOUT}=1\)$
    3. These 71 correctness failures and 1 timeout were explicitly preserved in documentation to prevent misrepresenting unverified modules as accelerated.
    4. Sub-1.0x Speedup & cuBLAS Superiority:
    5. fused_favor_plus: Triton implementation yields 0.73x speedup (0.098 ms Triton vs 0.071 ms PyTorch cuBLAS) on RTX 4070 (SM 8.9), documented in README.md Section 16 and verified in results/triton_all_26_kernels_benchmark.json (line 165).
    6. In results/triton_full_leaderboard_v136.csv, multiple Triton kernels and compression engines achieve \(<1.0\times\) speedup, including res_svd (0.01x), dynamic_svd (0.21x), dobi_svd (0.24x), a3_compressor (0.31x), linear_attn.py (0.20x), low_rank.py (0.25x), sparse_attn.py (0.26x), state_update.py (0.60x), and fused_ce.py (0.98x).
    7. In results/master_108_cuda_modules_benchmark.csv, paged_recurrent_step exhibits severe latency regression (CUDA 30.35 ms vs Ref 0.037 ms — 826x slower than PyTorch).
    8. Four Pseudo-Triton Functions:
    9. Four low-rank operations in neuralops/ops/triton/low_rank.py previously bore _triton suffixes despite executing PyTorch matrix multiplications and contractions. These were formally deprecated in v1.8.1 (CHANGELOG.md line 112, commit 3b00eeb09a):
      1. fused_nystrom_landmark_pinv_step_triton \(\rightarrow\) fused_nystrom_landmark_pinv_step_pytorch
      2. fused_rpc_sketch_attention_triton \(\rightarrow\) fused_rpc_sketch_attention_pytorch
      3. fused_primal_dual_landmark_triton \(\rightarrow\) fused_primal_dual_landmark_pytorch
      4. fused_svd_mamba_proj_triton \(\rightarrow\) fused_svd_mamba_proj_pytorch
    10. Hardware Wall & Out-of-Memory (OOM) Limits:
    11. Dense quadratic attention and FlashAttention-2 suffer hard CUDA OOM at sequence length \(N \ge 8192\) on 12 GB Ada Lovelace hardware (>22.4 GB required), grounding the theoretical necessity for linear attention and SSMs.
    12. Direct SVD & Naive Cross-Entropy Collapse:
    13. Direct SVD parameter reduction without activation whitening drops DistilBERT SST-2 accuracy to 34.80% and RoBERTa to 36.20% at \(r=4\) (worse than random guess, 50%).
    14. Post-surgery naive cross-entropy training collapses hidden state cosine similarity to \(<0.25\), establishing the mandatory requirement for 6-pillar knowledge distillation.

    3. Detailed Cross-Reference with Root Documentation

    3.1 README.md Sections 16, 17, and 18

    Section 16: Honest Performance Claims & Fused Kernel Spectrum

    • Textual Claim (README.md:1637-1655): Section 16 explicitly documents that not all Triton kernels outperform cuBLAS across every tensor shape. It partitions the 26 Triton kernel modules into:
    • High-Speedup Kernels (> 2.0x): 13 modules
    • Moderate-Speedup Kernels (1.0x–2.0x): 12 modules
    • Sub-Optimal / cuBLAS Preferred (< 1.0x): 1 module (fused_favor_plus at 0.73x)
    • Underlying Micro-Architectural Rationale: NVIDIA cuBLAS incorporates deeply hand-tuned assembly micro-pipelines. For small dimensions (\(D < 256\)), Triton JIT compiler launch overhead exceeds the raw arithmetic compute time. The platform implements automatic dispatch routing via check_triton_suitability() to redirect sub-256 shapes to PyTorch cuBLAS.
    • Empirical Grounding in results/: Directly validated in results/triton_all_26_kernels_benchmark.json (line 165), recording 0.098 ms (Triton) vs 0.071 ms (PyTorch cuBLAS), yielding 0.73x speedup on shape (256x64, M=128).

    Section 17: When NOT to Use NeuralOps

    • Textual Claim (README.md:1658-1667): Identifies five operational scenarios where NeuralOps is suboptimal:
    • Short Sequences (\(T < 512\)): Standard FlashAttention-2 is faster because the linear \(\mathcal{O}(T)\) constant overhead exceeds \(512^2\).
    • Sub-millisecond batch-1 serving: Specialized static C++ engines (TensorRT-LLM) outperform dynamic Python/Triton runtimes.
    • Pure weight-only quantization without surgery: Point tools (AutoAWQ, bitsandbytes) have lighter footprints.
    • Massive datacenter pretraining (>70B): Megatron-LM/DeepSpeed ZeRO-3 are required.
    • Legacy hardware (SM < 6.1): Compute architectures lacking Triton support fall back to unaccelerated reference paths.

    Section 18: Known Limitations & Failure Modes

    • Textual Claim (README.md:1670-1681):
    • Unactivated Gaussian Singularity: Raw Gaussian inputs (\(\mathbb{E}[x] = 0\)) into gla_scan.py cause output explosion if the denominator \(q_t Z_t + 10^{-4}\) crosses zero. Mitigated in v2.0.1 by forcing ELU(x) + 1.0 and hardware denominator clamping (tl.maximum(denom, 1e-4)).
    • Bidirectional Recurrence in Causal Models: Bidirectional scans (0.5 * (y_fwd + y_bwd)) violate autoregressive causality if applied to GPT-2/LLaMA decoders.
    • Small Batch GEMM Launch Overhead: High launch latency for \(B=1, T<128\).

      ### Known Limitations

    4. Architectural Synthesis & Methodological Value

    Far from undermining the credibility of the NeuralOps framework, these negative results and documented bottlenecks constitute its primary scientific strengths: 1. Validation of 3-Level Dispatch: If custom CUDA/Triton kernels were uniformly faster across all operations, the complex dynamic dispatch hierarchy (CUDA -> Triton -> PyTorch) would represent unnecessary engineering overhead. Slower kernels in memory-bound reduction regimes (fused_cross_entropy, fused_norm_linear) prove why dynamic routing to cuBLAS is essential. 2. Justification of HNC-SVD: The collapse of naive direct SVD (\(34.80\%\) accuracy) provides the empirical counterproof establishing why Cholesky activation whitening (\(\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{XX} = \mathbf{L}\mathbf{L}^\top\)) and Hessian null-space projection are theoretically required. 3. Necessity of 6-Pillar Distillation: The catastrophic loss of manifold structure under hard label cross-entropy (\(<0.25\) cosine similarity) provides the empirical justification for Stiefel-manifold and Wasserstein geometry preservation during post-surgery recovery. 4. Data Integrity Standard: All 71 correctness failures and empirical timeouts are preserved and cataloged, preventing silent regressions and upholding the zero-fake-artifact standard of the platform.
